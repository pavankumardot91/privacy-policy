Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Oct 6, 2026

At FLIPUR (accessible from flipur.trade), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by FLIPUR and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our trade-inverting execution tool, website, or browser extension, we may collect the following information:

  • Account & Contact Data: Email address, user name, and account preferences when you register or sign up for updates.
  • Technical & API Credentials: Encrypted broker/exchange API keys required solely to execute inverted orders on your connected trading account.
  • Trade Execution Logs: Anonymized timestamps, order trigger types, and inverted trade status for diagnostic and performance optimization purposes.
  • Usage Data: Information regarding how you interact with our website, browser extension, or application dashboard (e.g., IP address, browser type, device information).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our execution overlay and website services.
  • To process and execute real-time inverted order logic (BUY to SELL, SELL to BUY) as authorized by you.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand our software performance and user interface.
  • To communicate with you regarding account updates, security notices, technical support, and product launches.
  • To prevent fraud, security breaches, and illegal activity.

3. API Security & Credential Protection

Your broker and exchange API keys are encrypted using industry-standard protocols. FLIPUR does not store plain-text credentials, nor do we request permissions beyond what is necessary to place and manage trade orders as directed by you.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, FLIPUR uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We may share limited data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, conducting business, or serving our users, provided those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

6. Data Security

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information, username, password, transaction information, and data stored on our platform.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of the personal data we hold about you. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Privacy Policy Updates

FLIPUR reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage users to frequently check this page for any changes.
